Some time ago, I came across a post about Flat Earth. The post argued that if smoke from a train moving at 100 km/h leaves smoke _behind_, how come smoke (actually it looks more like steam) from the earth spinning at ~1600 km/h rises straight up?

![Train Smoke and Flat Earth](https://scontent.fblr1-4.fna.fbcdn.net/v/t1.0-9/67551466_10214024444060919_5092553430220668928_n.jpg?_nc_cat=110&_nc_oc=AQmeBE0ZHgfczDEUkpnGg_osER7e7rsY2olysu0JJE3APVY4DBynHcIsRpzKLm6FoAA&_nc_ht=scontent.fblr1-4.fna&oh=c3de2909eee9707a185314d621bcea96&oe=5DAFC9B2)

The Flat Earth theory is rather complex. The theory, by design, questions every little concept that we have learnt in physics---gravity, fluid dynamics, kinematics ... even basic mathematics. The Flat Earth Theorists pose these questions in such a way that we would lose our patience and stop arguing at a point. At this point, they would strut around like the proverbial pigeon on a chessboard.

## The argument

Here are some of the specific arguments made in the post:

1. If the earth were moving at the said 1,600 km/h, smoke rising up from the earth should 'go backwards', and not rise up straight.
2. No actual photo of the earth or a footage of it spinning or revolving exists.
3. We can feel the tiniest earthquake or the gentlest breeze. How do we not feel the speed at which we are rotating or revolving?
4. The rotation and revolution of the earth has never been actually measured.
5. Scientists revealed not long ago the hitherto hidden-from-us truth that the moon is in the earth's atmosphere.
6. The sun and the moon are the same size, and that is why a perfect solar eclipse is possible.
7. Adding a dash of religion, the argument says, the numbers associated with the spherical earth are the Satan's:
  - The speed of revolution of the earth around the sun: 66,600 miles/hour.
  - Curvature of one square mile of the earth: 0.666 ft.
  - The tilt angle of the earth: 6.66°
8. How come the earth moves steadily at 66,000 miles/hour around the sun while everything we throw slows and stops moving after a while?
9. The pattern of stars we see are the same every night, and this would not be possible if the earth were rotating.
10. Since humans measure angular speed, despite having the same linear speed, the lower angular speed of father-away objects make them seem to move slower.
11. The [earth seems flat from 100,000 miles above](https://youtu.be/wfUbBn7u2d0) the surface of the earth.
12. You can see Africa from Spain "for starters", which would not be possible if the earth curved.
13. Atmospheric thickness is what makes far-off objects disappear. With modern cameras, we can zoom in to see ships that have disappeared from view.

## Some questions answered by Flat Earth

The best artefact of all, is this map of the flat earth, including the sun and its orbit. It blew my mind. And I understood how their theory actually makes sense to them.

![The Flat Earth map along with the Sun and its orbit](https://wiki.tfes.org/images/c/c7/Voliva_map.jpg)

The theory answers a small set of logical questions. For instance:

1. How does all the water from the oceans fall off, if the earth were flat? Because the ice rim acts as the bund.
2. How come no one has seen the end of the flat earth? Because nobody has managed to cross the ice rim.
3. How do we have day and night? Because the sun is 27 miles across and its orbit almost overlaps with the equator. As it goes about its orbit, its light falls concentrated on the area right below the sun.
4. How do we not see the sun in the night? Atmospheric thickness. The cloud and fog and all blocks the sun from view when it goes far away; for example, you are in Africa, and the sun is above Australia, it's far away from you.
5. How do you manage to go from Europe to North America one way, and you come back to the other side of Europe while travelling on the same line? Because you can never go straight. What you think is going straight is actually circle. This is why humans measure speed as angular speed and not linear speed. This is the reason for parallax as well. What your eye perceives is a circular field.

But there are questions the model fails to answer. For instance:

1. If we measure angular distance, how does it work when we are traversing the longitudes? For instance, when travelling from Asia to Australia. Or do we measure angular distance when travelling along latitudes and linear when travelling along the longitudes?
2. With the arguments above in mind, how do we explain the phenomenon where we are in a plane, and don't feel like I am moving at all, while in reality, I am travelling at over 800 km/h? At that speed, I should be feeling dizzy.
3. The aeroplane is travelling at half the speed of the earth. How come the steam from my coffee rises straight up?
4. Assuming that the moon is at a lower level compared to the sun, and when the two overlap is what causes solar eclipse, how do we explain the lunar eclipse? Whose shadow is falling on the moon? If the moon is a source of light in itself, why is it always dark during solar eclipse?
5. How to we explain the curvature in the phases of the moon?
6. I am a Hindu, so, I don't have to believe in the number 666. Convenient for me. No questions there, of course.
7. Why does it so happen that based on the time of the year, the Big Dipper seem to rotate around the Pole Star?
8. When I place my camera on super-low exposure and set it to capture time-lapse, I see concentric circles in a starry sky? What is rotating to produce that?
9. If far-off objects seem to move slower than the nearby objects, and that far-off objects seem smaller than the nearby objects, how can the sun and the moon be of the same size and yet, be not on the same plane?
10. Why is it so surprising that we can see African hilltops from Spain, if the two are a mere fifteen or so kilometres apart? (The Strait of Gibraltar separates the two, and as long as the hills are over 20 metres or taller, we should be able to see them.)
11. How do we explain the Coriolis Effect?
12. How does half the world receive sunlight at any given time, and the other half doesn't? In a flat earth, the sunlight would be a spot (imagine the spotlight); the land beyond the spot should be dark.
13. How do we explain the changing seasons?
14. How do we explain the centre of mass when we are talking about a plain, and then, how do we explain gravity?

I will stop at this for now.
