There are perks of being an author. Like, you are seen as someone who knows his stuff[^peer-review]. But then, there are demerits as well, like people asking you for a free copy of your book. I thought I'd share my thoughts on it a little, today.

[^peer-review]: It's bad practice to simply assume someone as knowing their stuff just because they wrote a book, in general. You should ideally see how the book has held up in terms of peer reviews.
